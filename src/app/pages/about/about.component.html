About My Choice Interior

We turn houses into homes that feel like you

For over a decade, we’ve designed and delivered interiors that balance beauty, function and value — for families across India.

Our Story

Design that respects your time, budget and trust

My Choice Interior began with a simple belief: getting your home done shouldn’t be stressful. Too many families juggle carpenters, vendors and unclear quotes. We changed that by bringing design, premium materials and execution under one accountable roof.

Today, our studio blends thoughtful design with factory-grade precision — and backs every project with transparent pricing, regular updates and lasting post-work support.

Our Mission

To make premium, transparent, on-time interior design accessible to every home — with quality you can name and trust you can feel.

Our Vision

To be India’s most trusted interior partner — the studio families recommend for craftsmanship, honesty and care that lasts beyond handover.
